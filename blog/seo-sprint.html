Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): The New SEO Frontier

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): The New SEO Frontier

Growth strategy sprint from the Samraddhi Marketing Team.

The Shift from SEO to GEO

Search engines are evolving. With the rise of Google's AI Overviews, SearchGPT, Perplexity, and Claude, users are increasingly getting direct answers to their queries rather than a list of links. To stay visible, brands must transition from traditional Search Engine Optimization (SEO) to Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).

How LLMs Retrieve Information

Large Language Models (LLMs) search the web to synthesize answers. They prioritize websites that feature clean structured data, cite authoritative statistics, present information in logical Q&A formats, and maintain consistent brand mentions across multiple web repositories.

Actionable Steps to Optimize for AI Search

  • Structure Content for Q&A: Use H2/H3 headings that directly ask common questions, followed by concise, factual answers.
  • Cite Authentic Data: Generative engines rely heavily on sources that cite peer-reviewed studies, original research, or specific data points.
  • Implement Clean Schema: Ensure your website uses extensive Schema.org JSON-LD markup to explain exactly who you are, what services you offer, and your local location details.
